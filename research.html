Research Overview

Nuclear Balance Relations (NBT) is an independent research project investigating a compact phenomenological description of systematic neutron excess in atomic nuclei.

The project is motivated by the systematic behavior of neutron-to-proton balance in nuclei and by the physical considerations represented in the Semi-Empirical Mass Formula (SEMF).

NBT-1

The first proposed relation describes neutron number as a function of proton number:

N = Z + kZ5/3

The coefficient k is determined through numerical fitting to the selected AME2020 nuclear dataset.

NBT-2

A second formulation adds an additional power-law term:

N = Z + k1Z5/3 + k2Z2/3

NBT-2 was investigated as an extended phenomenological model and compared quantitatively with NBT-1.

Numerical Validation

The analysis uses nuclear mass data from AME2020. For each proton number Z, the isotope with the largest experimental binding energy per nucleon was selected, subject to the available dataset.

The analysis includes goodness-of-fit measures such as RMSE and MAE, together with comparison between the compact NBT relations and the Semi-Empirical Mass Formula.

Scientific Status

NBT is presented as a phenomenological description rather than a replacement for established nuclear models. Its purpose is to investigate whether a compact mathematical relation can reproduce an important systematic trend in nuclear composition.
